# 60-Second Storyboard

## Concept
**Tomorrow Has a View**

Nine controlled shots. Approximate durations may shift during edit, but total master must be 58–62 seconds.

### SH01 — The Landmark Appears — 0:00–0:06
**Purpose:** Immediate scale and surprise.
- Golden-hour aerial over Mazar urban context.
- Camera banks gently and reveals the completed Mazar Mall.
- Building must read instantly as the supplied architecture.
- Minimal people/traffic motion; believable city life.
**Transition:** Camera continues toward facade.

### SH02 — Street-Level Presence — 0:06–0:12
**Purpose:** Make the CGI feel built and inhabitable.
- Low, elegant street-level dolly / vehicle-height glide.
- Commercial podium active but not crowded.
- Reflections, trees, pavement, cars, warm interiors.
- Reveal podium materials and entrance scale.
**Transition:** Camera tilts/rises with facade lines.

### SH03 — Vertical Rise / 18-Floor Reveal — 0:12–0:19
**Purpose:** Communicate height and residential tower identity.
- Controlled vertical crane/drone move.
- Travel from commercial base past amenities to residential balconies.
- Do not count floors with text; architecture should remain stable.
**Transition:** Ease toward Floor 5 balcony.

### SH04 — Arrival at Floor 5 — 0:19–0:27
**Purpose:** Move from landmark to human experience.
- Camera approaches the **second residential floor (Floor 5)** balcony.
- Resident seen in silhouette/three-quarter profile.
- Coffee in hand.
- Golden sunlight catches glass edge and balcony rail.
**Transition:** Slow push-in; soundscape softens.

### SH05 — Signature Sunset Moment — 0:27–0:38
**Purpose:** Emotional core / “I want to live here.”
- Resident quietly watches sunset.
- Subtle coffee steam.
- Warm rim light, realistic skin/cloth, gentle wind.
- City depth beyond balcony.
- Optional close insert: hand + coffee + reflected sunset, not face.
- Hold longer than other shots.
**Transition:** Camera drifts backward through open balcony doors.

### SH06 — Home Revealed — 0:38–0:45
**Purpose:** Connect the view to residential quality.
- Seamless backward move into a premium furnished living space.
- Interior language can borrow from supplied furnished unit-plan references.
- Architecture remains plausible; no impossible room morphing.
- Resident remains background silhouette if visible.
**Transition:** Match-cut on warm linear lighting / reflective surface.

### SH07 — Lifestyle Pulse — 0:45–0:51
**Purpose:** Show mixed-use value without becoming a brochure montage.
Three micro-beats inside one 6-second designed sequence:
- refined lobby / arrival;
- lifestyle / amenity glimpse on Floor 3;
- commercial evening activity.
Use elegant match cuts, not rapid social-media edits.
**Transition:** Exterior light source becomes night facade lighting.

### SH08 — Golden Hour to Blue Hour — 0:51–0:56
**Purpose:** “Landmark after dark.”
- Exterior orbit or pull-back.
- Time-of-day transitions naturally from sunset to blue hour.
- Building lights wake progressively.
- Preserve exact geometry.
**Transition:** Settle into final architectural hero angle.

### SH09 — Night Hero / Brand Close — 0:56–1:00
**Purpose:** Leave a premium final impression.
- Stable wide night hero.
- Minimal camera drift.
- Logo/title enters cleanly.
Suggested:
  MAZAR MALL
  Tomorrow Has a View.
- No unapproved phone, price, claim, or sales CTA.

## Sound arc
- SH01: restrained cinematic rise + city air.
- SH02–03: low architectural pulse, subtle traffic.
- SH04: music opens / breathes.
- SH05: emotional drop, wind + city distance + cup detail.
- SH06–07: elegant rhythm returns.
- SH08: controlled crescendo.
- SH09: resolved final chord / sonic logo.

Music and sound effects must be licensed or generated with rights suitable for intended use.
