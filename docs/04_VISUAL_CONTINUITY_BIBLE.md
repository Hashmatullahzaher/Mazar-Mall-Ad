# Visual Continuity Bible

Consistency is the highest-priority technical problem.

## Building lock
Every shot containing Mazar Mall must preserve:
- massing;
- floor rhythm;
- balcony curvature;
- facade color/material families;
- vertical dark elements;
- podium pattern;
- crown / top silhouette;
- major signage placement.

A beautiful shot with the wrong building FAILS.

## Reference strategy
For each exterior generation:
1. Use 2–4 strongest canonical exterior references, not random references.
2. Include at least one view close to the requested camera angle.
3. Use a clean start frame whenever image-to-video improves identity.
4. Preserve selected start frames as immutable continuity anchors.
5. Record every reference path in the shot manifest.

## Character lock
For SH04–SH06:
- same adult character;
- same clothing;
- same coffee cup/glass;
- same balcony;
- same sunset direction;
- same time-of-day progression.

Avoid face-dominant shots. Architectural lifestyle realism matters more than celebrity-grade face identity.

## Floor 5 lock
The balcony must visually correspond to the second residential floor:
- Floor 4 = residential level 1.
- Floor 5 = residential level 2.
Do not accidentally place the character near the top of the building.

## Lighting lock
Master arc:
- SH01–03: late golden hour.
- SH04–06: richest sunset.
- SH07: transition.
- SH08: blue hour.
- SH09: refined night.

Sun direction must remain broadly coherent across sequential exterior shots.

## Lens language
Suggested equivalents:
- Aerial establishing: 28–35mm.
- Street architectural: 24–32mm with controlled verticals.
- Facade rise: 28–40mm.
- Balcony approach: 50–70mm feel.
- Lifestyle detail: 70–100mm feel.
- Interior: 24–35mm, no ultra-wide distortion.
- Final hero: 35–50mm.

## Motion rules
- One primary camera move per shot.
- No jitter.
- No sudden acceleration unless designed as transition.
- Avoid impossible “AI swoops” through solid geometry.
- Use speed ramps only as edit decisions, not as a substitute for stable generation.

## Material rules
- White surfaces retain detail; never blown-out plastic.
- Glass reflects environment but remains transparent where appropriate.
- Gold podium material should read architectural, not jewelry-like.
- Night lighting warm and integrated, not neon cyberpunk.

## People / vehicles
- Scale must be realistic.
- Background people are secondary.
- Cars must not morph.
- Avoid excessive crowds that create generation errors.

## Negative constraint library
Use selectively:
- no architecture morphing
- no extra floors
- no missing balconies
- no warped windows
- no floating objects
- no duplicate people
- no deformed hands
- no text mutation
- no logo mutation
- no fisheye distortion
- no oversaturated HDR
- no cyberpunk neon
- no fantasy skyline
- no camera jitter
- no unstable facade geometry
