# Creative Brief — Mazar Mall 60s CGI Film

## Objective
Create a 60-second cinematic CGI advertisement for a private client presentation that makes the viewer emotionally experience the completed Mazar Mall before construction is complete.

The target reaction is:
> “This looks like a finished landmark, not an AI concept.”

## Audience
Primary: Mazar Mall developer / decision maker.
Secondary: future buyers, investors, partners, and premium tenants if the concept later becomes public-facing.

## Central idea
**TOMORROW HAS A VIEW.**

Mazar Mall is presented as a future already lived: active streets, warm commercial life, elegant vertical arrival, and a quiet residential sunset above Mazar-e-Sharif.

## Emotional arc
1. **Awe** — reveal the landmark.
2. **Believability** — prove scale, materials, access, and human activity.
3. **Desire** — show what it feels like to live there.
4. **Intimacy** — slow down for the coffee-on-balcony sunset signature moment.
5. **Confidence** — show lifestyle / amenity value.
6. **Legacy** — night hero and brand close.

## Signature image
A resident on the balcony of **Floor 5 (second residential floor)**, holding a glass/cup of coffee, quietly watching the sunset over Mazar. The camera, lighting, wind, reflection, and sound design should make this the emotional center of the film.

## Style
- Premium architectural visualization.
- Photoreal CGI, not “AI fantasy.”
- Physically plausible scale, glass, stone, metal, shadows, people, and traffic.
- Restrained luxury.
- Warm golden hour transitioning into blue hour / night.
- Elegant camera movement, never frantic.
- Real Mazar Mall geometry and facade identity are more important than novelty.

## Camera language
- Controlled drone / crane / dolly / gimbal language.
- One or two “impossible” seamless transitions are allowed, but they must still feel cinematic.
- Avoid constant FPV speed, fisheye distortion, or warping architecture.
- Prefer 24fps cinematic motion with realistic shutter/motion blur in final edit.

## Human direction
The balcony character is not the “star.” The property is the star.
- Natural posture.
- Premium but ordinary clothing.
- Coffee held casually.
- No direct look at camera.
- Subtle breathing and hand movement.
- Light wind on clothing/hair.
- No uncanny facial closeup.

## Brand close
Suggested end line:
**MAZAR MALL**
**Tomorrow Has a View.**

Alternative:
**MAZAR MALL — Live the Future.**

Final tagline remains subject to client approval.

## What not to do
- No fake brands on shops.
- No unverified claims (“largest,” “first,” “best”) unless approved.
- No invented site skyline presented as exact reality.
- No visibly morphing balconies, windows, floors, signage, or unit layouts.
- No generic towers replacing the supplied Mazar Mall identity.
- No 60-second single generation. Build the film as controlled short shots.
