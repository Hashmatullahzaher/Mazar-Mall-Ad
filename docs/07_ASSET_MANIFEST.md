## Required site material
`assets/site/`

For exact-site CGI compositing, add at least:
- wide site photo from street;
- opposite-angle site photo;
- drone/roof-level site view if possible;
- sunset-direction reference;
- short stable drone or phone clips if available;
- any known site survey / geolocation image.

Until then:
`SITE_MATCH_UNVERIFIED`
